Anaconda, Montana

Two grain silos and a cottage, five minutes from downtown.

Wildroot Silos is a small group of modern stays built inside converted grain silos, with a separate cottage alongside. Spend the day at Discovery Ski Hill or Georgetown Lake, then come back to the hot tub.
